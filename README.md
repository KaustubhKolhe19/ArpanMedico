# ARPAN MEDICO — Wholesale Medical & Surgical Supplies

**Arpan Medico** is a leading authorized wholesale distributor of genuine MNC pharmaceuticals, surgical instruments, IV fluids, and hospital consumables based in **Sangamner, Maharashtra, India**.

Under the visionary leadership of **Milan Pradip Fargade**, Arpan Medico serves multi-specialty hospitals, nursing homes, practitioner clinics, diagnostic laboratories, and retail pharmacies with guaranteed 100% genuine medical supplies, wholesale rates, and rapid order dispatch.

---

## 🏥 Business Overview & Core Capabilities

- **Authorized MNC Healthcare Partner**: Supplying top-tier medical brands including Nipro, Ethicon, Polymed, BD (Becton Dickinson), Romsons, Healthium, Nulife, and other leading manufacturers.
- **Wholesale Pharmaceutical Distribution**: Bulk supply of essential prescription medicines, IV fluids, critical care items, and temperature-controlled medical inventory.
- **Surgical Products & Consumables**: Certified sutures, surgical needles, IV cannulas, syringes, catheters, sterile surgical gloves, wound care, and dressing supplies.
- **Medical Equipment & Instruments**: Hospital furniture, diagnostic devices, orthopedic supports, wheelchairs, walkers, and patient care instruments.
- **Distribution Radius**: Central wholesale hub serving Sangamner, Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar) district, and surrounding regions across Maharashtra.

## 🌐 Digital Web Platform Features

This project represents the official digital B2B web application for **Arpan Medico**, designed with executive UI aesthetics, rich interactive components, and search engine optimization.

1. **Executive B2B Showcase**:
   - Modern glassmorphic Hero section with trust badges and immediate WhatsApp inquiry triggers.
   - 4-Card Offerings Grid covering Wholesale Distribution, Medicines & IV Fluids, Surgical Products, and Medical Equipment.
   - Interactive 6-Card Specialty Grid highlighting verified business capabilities.

2. **Partner Brands Showcase**:
   - 7-Column Brand Grid showcasing authorized MNC healthcare manufacturers with direct inquiry links.

3. **Interactive Local Presence Hub**:
   - Live embedded Google Maps interactive container displaying the physical distribution warehouse at Kadlag Complex, New Nagar Road, Sangamner.
   - One-click Google Maps directions and direct hotline dialing.

4. **Business Intelligence & Performance Analytics**:
   - Interactive analytics dashboard highlighting order volumes, monthly revenue growth, and category demand distribution.

   - Fixed floating WhatsApp contact widget with custom pre-filled inquiry text generator.
   - Quick direct-dial actions across desktop and mobile devices.

- **Search Meta Tags**: Custom titles, meta descriptions, and keywords optimized for Sangamner, Ahilyanagar, and Maharashtra healthcare wholesale searches.
